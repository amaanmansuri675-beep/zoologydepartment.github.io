About the Field Visit

The field visit provided practical knowledge about poultry production and scientific management practices.

🐔 Poultry Farming

Poultry farming is the scientific rearing and management of domestic birds such as chickens for the production of eggs and meat.

During the field visit, different aspects of poultry production were observed, including housing, feeding, breeding, chick management, egg collection and hygiene.

The visit helped students understand how theoretical knowledge of Zoology is applied in practical poultry production.

Practical Learning

Poultry Birds

Chicken Varieties

Important poultry categories observed and studied during the field visit.

MEAT PURPOSE

Broiler

Broilers are chickens specially selected and reared for rapid growth and efficient meat production.

EGG PURPOSE

Layer

Layer birds are maintained primarily for high and regular egg production.

BREEDING PURPOSE

Breeder

Breeder birds are selected and managed to produce fertile eggs for hatchery and chick production.

INDIGENOUS

Desi / Indigenous

Indigenous chickens are adapted to local environmental conditions and are commonly reared under backyard or semi-intensive systems.

YOUNG BIRD

Chicks

Newly hatched chicks require proper brooding, temperature control, clean water, balanced feed and disease prevention.

HATCHERY

Hatching Eggs

Fertile eggs from healthy breeder birds are carefully collected, stored and incubated for chick production.

Scientific Practices

Poultry Management

Major management practices required for healthy birds and efficient poultry production.

🌾

Feed & Nutrition

Birds should receive balanced feed containing carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals and adequate clean water.

🏠

Poultry Shed

Proper ventilation, lighting, temperature, space and cleanliness are essential for maintaining healthy poultry birds.

💉

Vaccination

Vaccination programmes and biosecurity practices help protect poultry from important infectious diseases.

🧼

Hygiene & Biosecurity

Regular cleaning, disinfection, controlled entry and proper waste disposal reduce disease risks.

🌡️

Temperature Control

Young chicks need careful temperature management during brooding to maintain comfort and survival.

📊

Record Keeping

Records of feed consumption, mortality, body weight, egg production and health help monitor farm performance.

Special Study

Broiler & Breeder Management

Understanding the different objectives of meat production and breeding programmes.

🍗 Broiler Management

Broiler production focuses on rapid and efficient growth. Proper nutrition, ventilation, temperature, clean water and disease prevention are important.

Regular observation of body weight, feed intake, behaviour and health helps maintain good flock performance.

🐔 Breeder Management

Breeder management focuses on maintaining healthy male and female birds capable of producing high-quality fertile eggs.

Proper feeding, body-weight control, flock health and egg collection are important parts of breeder management.

Conclusion
